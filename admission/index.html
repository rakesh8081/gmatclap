Consulting Process
  1. Initial Profile Evaluation:
    • Embark on a comprehensive, introspective call to evaluate your strengths, weaknesses, and mutual fit for the application process. Lay the foundation for a personalized strategy tailored to your unique profile.
  2. Identifying Your Key Strengths:
    • Utilize our proven frameworks to pinpoint your strengths, values, and unique attributes that set you apart. Craft a narrative that authentically represents your distinctive qualities.
  3. Discovering Special YOU:
    • Construct a compelling career story that transcends the ordinary. Unearth the milestones, challenges, accomplishments, and leadership stories that define your professional journey.
  4. Finding Your Career Goals:
    • Clearly articulate your short- and long-term goals. Align your aspirations with the ethos of top-notch business schools.
  5. Researching & Finalizing Best-Fit Schools:
    • Collaboratively create a curated list of best-fit schools. Leverage our frameworks and resources for in-depth research, empowering you to make informed decisions about your academic future.
  6. Choosing Your Recommenders:
    • Receive guidance on selecting the right referees strategically. Craft impactful recommendations that highlight your achievements and contributions.
  7. Resume Building:
    • Engage in an intensive 1-1 workshop focused on creating a resume that strategically highlights your strengths and achievements. Develop a document that serves as a compelling testament to your professional journey.
  8. Reviewing Your Essays:
    • Collaborate on crafting compelling essays that align seamlessly with admissions expectations. Benefit from unlimited reviews and edits, ensuring your essays are a true reflection of your strengths and personality.
