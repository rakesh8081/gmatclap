Success story

GMAT scores

Admits

Besides the admits above, Tanu Gupta was admitted to ISB's YL programme.

Different backgrounds, one programme

The list ranges from an engineer with 3 years of experience to a professional with 17, and from Indian to European graduates. Every GMATclap student follows the same programme:

  • A diagnostic test first, so the study plan is built around each student's weak areas.
  • Official (OG) questions solved and discussed in class, more than 3,000 of them.
  • Topic tests and full-length mock tests with detailed analysis.
  • Unlimited revision classes for topics that need another pass.
