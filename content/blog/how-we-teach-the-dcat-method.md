Most GMAT students do not fail for lack of effort. They fail because they study everything with the same intensity, solve questions without learning from them, and take their first realistic mock test far too late. Our D-C-A-T method is built to avoid exactly those mistakes.

## D – Demo and Diagnosis

Before we teach anything, we find out where you stand. A free demo class shows you how we teach, and a diagnostic test shows us your strengths and gaps in Quant, Verbal and Data Insights. Your plan is built from that, not from a one-size-fits-all syllabus.

## C – Concept

Every topic is taught from first principles in live classes on Zoom, along with the tricks and shortcuts that save time on the real exam. The full course covers about 60 hours of Quant, Verbal and Data Insights classes, plus doubt-clearing sessions.

## A – Application

A concept only counts once you can use it under time pressure. We share, solve and discuss **more than 3,000 official (OG) questions** in class, so you learn how the GMAT actually tests each idea.

## T – Test

You test early and often: **130+ topic tests**, unlimited AI-generated practice tests and **6 full-length mock tests**, each with detailed analysis and solutions. Mistakes found in a mock are mistakes you will not make on test day.

## What else comes with it

- **Small batches**, so every student gets attention.
- **Unlimited revision classes**, at no extra fee.
- **Hand-holding until you succeed on the GMAT.** If you want to prepare again, you can attend classes in a later batch for free.
- Complete study material and notes.
